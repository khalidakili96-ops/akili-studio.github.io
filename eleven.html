The Concept

A retail environment designed to make discovery feel effortless.

Eleven translates the energy of children's fashion into a clear, contemporary retail experience. The design balances playful character with disciplined circulation, display and material choices, allowing the products to remain the focus while the space develops its own identity.

Retail Interior Design

Retail Interior Design in Cairo, where circulation and identity shape the experience.

Eleven approaches retail interior design as an experience of movement, discovery and display. The spatial arrangement supports clear circulation while giving the children's fashion collection a distinctive environment with a strong sense of identity.

Project TypeCommercial · Retail

Design Resolution

Playful in character. Precise in structure.

The interior uses scale, framing and controlled visual rhythm to create an environment that feels engaging without becoming visually chaotic. Display zones, circulation and focal moments are treated as one coherent retail system.

Retail with identity

A space that turns a simple visit into an experience.

Eleven reflects Akili Studio's approach to commercial interiors: a strong identity supported by clear spatial logic, considered detailing and an experience designed around the people who use it.
