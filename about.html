About Akili Studio

Made for the way
you live.

We believe thoughtful design should feel as natural as it is beautiful.

Our Approach

Quietly confident spaces, deeply personal in character.

Akili Studio is an interior architecture and design practice based in Cairo. We create spaces that carry a strong point of view without ever overwhelming the people who live in them. Our process brings together spatial intelligence, a sensitive material palette and meticulous execution.

What We Value

Beauty with purpose.

Every choice has a reason: to improve a daily ritual, create a moment of calm, bring light into a room or make a home feel entirely its own. This is the lasting value of considered design.
