Welcome to Akili Studio

Where identity meets space. Designed with intention.

Akili Studio is an interior design studio creating refined, purposeful spaces shaped around the people who experience them.

Founded by architect and designer Khaled Akili, the studio combines spatial thinking, materiality, and architectural precision to create interiors that balance beauty with function.

From private residences to commercial spaces, every project is approached with clarity and care — creating environments that feel personal, considered, and built to endure.

Our Design Philosophy

Every decision has a purpose.

At Akili Studio, we believe good design begins with understanding. Every project starts by considering how people live, what they value, and what the space needs to achieve.

We bring together identity, functionality, and refined aesthetics to create interiors that feel personal and purposeful. Every material, proportion, detail, and light source is considered for both its visual quality and its impact on everyday experience.

We do not design for trends. We create spaces with clarity and character — carefully shaped to function naturally, reflect their owners, and remain relevant over time.

Vision

A globally recognised studio with a timeless point of view.

To become a globally recognised interior design studio known for creating timeless, emotionally intelligent spaces that reflect identity, elevate everyday living, and unite elegance with precision.

Mission

We design with intention.

Our mission is to create personalised spaces that balance beauty, functionality, and identity. Through thoughtful design, material expertise, and architectural discipline, we guide each project from concept to execution with clarity, care, and consistency.

Project Completion Method

From first understanding
to final execution.

A considered five-stage method keeps the design coherent, purposeful and aligned from the first conversation to the finished space.

01

Discovery

We understand the client, the space, the lifestyle, requirements and objectives before defining the design direction.

02

Moodboard

We establish the visual language through atmosphere, materials, colour, texture and references.

03

Layouts

We shape the spatial organisation, circulation, furniture planning and functional relationships.

04

3D Visualisation

We translate the design into detailed visualisations that communicate the intended space, materials and atmosphere.

05

Execution

We carry the design into reality through technical coordination, site supervision and careful attention to the final details.
