Welcome to Akili Studio

Design begins
with understanding.

Akili Studio creates refined, purposeful interiors shaped around the people who experience them. Founded by architect and designer Khaled Akili, the studio brings spatial thinking, materiality and architectural precision together.

Every project begins before the drawing. We understand the client, the space and the ambition — then build a design language around it.

Our Design Philosophy

02 — Intention
Every element
has a reason.

We bring together identity, functionality and refined aesthetics to create interiors that feel personal and purposeful.

Material, proportion, detail and light are considered not only for how they look, but for how they change the experience of a space.

We do not design for trends. We create spaces with clarity and character — carefully shaped to remain relevant over time.
