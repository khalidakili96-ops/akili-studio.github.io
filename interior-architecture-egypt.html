Interior Architecture in Egypt

Akili Studio integrates spatial planning, architectural detail and interior design to create spaces that are coherent from structure to finish.

The Foundation

Architecture and interior design should begin from the same spatial logic.

Interior architecture sits between the building and the finished interior. It is where proportion, circulation, thresholds, openings, ceiling conditions, joinery, lighting and materiality are resolved as one environment. Akili Studio uses this architectural layer to give interiors greater clarity, function and permanence.

What We Resolve

Structure before decoration.

The strongest interiors are not assembled by adding finishes to a room. They are developed by understanding the space itself and making the architectural decisions that allow everything else to follow naturally.

01 / SPACE

Spatial Planning

Layouts, circulation, zoning and relationships between spaces are developed around function and experience.

02 / PROPORTION

Architectural Detail

Openings, walls, ceilings, built-ins and transitions are considered as part of one architectural composition.

03 / MATERIAL

Materiality

Stone, timber, metal, fabric, glass and other finishes are selected for their role in the overall spatial language.

04 / REALISATION

Buildability

Design decisions are developed with technical coordination and construction realities in mind from the outset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Interior Architecture in Egypt

What is the difference between interior architecture and interior design?

Interior architecture focuses more deeply on the spatial and architectural structure of an interior — including planning, proportions, circulation, openings, ceilings and built elements. Interior design develops the material, lighting, furniture and visual character of that environment. The two disciplines often overlap.

Does Akili Studio provide interior architecture in Cairo?

Yes. Akili Studio is based in Cairo and develops interior architecture and interior design projects in Cairo and elsewhere in Egypt, subject to project scope and requirements.

Can interior architecture be part of a residential project?

Yes. Interior architecture can be particularly valuable in apartments, villas and private residences where spatial planning, built-in elements and architectural details strongly affect how the home functions and feels.

Does interior architecture include construction drawings?

Depending on the agreed scope, interior architecture may include detailed drawings and coordinated information required to develop and execute the approved design.
