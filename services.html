Our Services

The space,
considered as a whole.

From spatial strategy and interior design to execution and selected brand identity work, we connect the decisions that shape a project — so the idea remains clear from first conversation to final detail.

The Akili Approach

Design is not a sequence of isolated decisions.

We structure the space before we refine it. Proportion, circulation, materiality, light, function and detail are developed as one system — creating a clear design direction that can be carried through to what is built.

01 / DESIGN

Interior Architecture & Interior Design

We develop complete interior environments around the people, architecture and purpose of each project — from spatial planning and concept development to materials, lighting, furniture and detailed design. Every decision is resolved as part of one coherent system, creating a space that feels considered, personal and built to endure.

Typical scope

Spatial planning · Concept direction · Materiality · Lighting · Furniture · Detailed design

02 / REALISATION

Execution & Supervision

We remain involved through realisation, coordinating specialists, reviewing workmanship, resolving site conditions and protecting the integrity of the approved design. The objective is straightforward: the quality, proportions and details agreed in design should carry through to what is built.

What we protect

Design intent · Quality · Coordination · Site decisions · Programme clarity

03 / BRAND

Brand Identity

For selected hospitality, retail and lifestyle projects, we develop visual identities that extend the physical experience into the brand itself. The same principles of clarity, proportion and intention guide the identity, creating a coherent relationship between the space, the customer experience and the brand.

Typical scope

Brand strategy · Visual direction · Identity systems · Typography · Colour · Brand applications
