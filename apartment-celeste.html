Apartment Celeste

Interior DesignResidentialContemporary

The Concept

Light, proportion and effortless permanence.

The design brief centred on creating a contemporary home without relying on a short-lived visual language. The architecture remains legible, while softer furnishings, measured contrast and carefully framed natural light give the interior a quieter sense of permanence.

Design Resolution

A home that stays calm as life changes around it.

The result is contemporary without being transient: a balanced interior organised around daily life, with proportion and material restraint doing more of the visual work than decoration.
