The Approach

Interior Design Shaped Around the Way You Live

Good interior design begins before materials, furniture or decoration. It begins with understanding how a space needs to function. At Akili Studio, every project is developed around its architecture, proportions, circulation, natural light, material palette and the people who will inhabit it.

A Complete Interior Design Process

01 / SPATIAL PLANNING

Spatial Planning

We establish layouts, circulation, proportions and functional relationships before the aesthetic layer is developed.

02 / INTERIOR DESIGN

Interior Design

Materials, lighting, furniture, colour and architectural details are composed into a unified interior language.

03 / TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

Technical Development

Design decisions are translated into drawings, specifications and coordinated information required to move towards construction.

04 / REALISATION

Execution & Supervision

Where Akili Studio is appointed for realisation, we coordinate with relevant trades and monitor execution to protect the approved design.

Designed Before It Is Built

One process. From first brief to finished space.

Interior design decisions become expensive when they are made too late. We resolve the space progressively — beginning with its structure, developing its material and visual identity, then translating the design into information that can be executed.

01

Understand

We establish the brief, lifestyle, requirements, architecture and ambitions.

02

Structure

Plans, proportions, circulation and spatial relationships define the foundation.

03

Refine

Materials, lighting, furniture, joinery and details develop the character.

04

Realise

Documentation, coordination and execution oversight carry the design into the physical environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Interior Design in Cairo

What does an interior design studio in Cairo typically handle?

An interior design studio can manage different stages of a project depending on the agreed scope, from spatial planning and concept development through material selection, detailed design, documentation and execution supervision.

Does Akili Studio work on residential projects?

Yes. Akili Studio works on residential interiors including apartments, private residences and villas.

Does Akili Studio work on commercial interiors?

Yes. The studio develops commercial interiors including retail and other customer-facing environments where spatial design and brand identity need to work together.

Can Akili Studio work with clients outside Cairo?

Yes. The design process can be structured for projects elsewhere in Egypt and for selected international projects, depending on the project's requirements and agreed scope.

Does Akili Studio provide execution supervision?

Execution and supervision can be included as part of the agreed project scope where required.

How do I start an interior design project with Akili Studio?

The process begins with a project enquiry covering the property, location, approximate size, requirements and intended scope. Akili Studio can then determine the appropriate next step.
