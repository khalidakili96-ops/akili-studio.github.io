The Approach

Before the material, there is the space.

Akili Studio treats interior design as a complete spatial discipline. We begin with the brief, understand the architecture and establish the right relationships between proportion, circulation, light, material and function.

Only then do we refine the visual language. The result is an interior that feels resolved rather than decorated.
