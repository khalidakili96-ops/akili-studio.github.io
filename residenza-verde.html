Residenza Verde

Residential InteriorInterior DesignTimeless Journey

The Concept

A composed residence shaped by material, proportion and atmosphere.

Residenza Verde explores the quieter side of residential design — bringing spatial clarity, tactile materiality and carefully controlled visual rhythm together to create a home that feels considered without becoming overstated.

Project TypeResidential
Primary ServiceInterior Design
Design FocusMateriality · Proportion · Continuity
Project PositioningTimeless Journey

Design Resolution

Continuity creates calm.

The design is resolved through a measured relationship between architecture, furniture, material and light. Each space carries its own character while remaining part of a coherent residential language.

A considered journey

Designed as a home, resolved as a whole.

Residenza Verde reflects Akili Studio's approach to residential interiors: thoughtful planning, refined material choices and precise decisions that allow a space to feel personal and enduring.
